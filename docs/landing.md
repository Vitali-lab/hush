## English

### Hero

**Hush**

# Speak softly. We'll do the typing.

On-device dictation for macOS. Hold a shortcut, say what you mean, release — your
words appear at the cursor in any app. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

**Download for macOS** → https://github.com/Vitali-lab/hush/releases/latest/download/Hush-macOS-arm64.dmg

*macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon · 632 MB model downloads once*

### Features

**Works in every app.** Slack, Mail, Cursor, Terminal, a browser — if you can type
there, you can dictate there. The text is pasted at the focused cursor.

**Stays on your Mac.** Whisper large-v3 Turbo runs on the Apple Neural Engine.
Audio is recorded in memory, transcribed locally, and never uploaded.

**Fast and light.** The model loads in under two seconds and uses roughly
130–200 MB of RAM while it works. It unloads itself after eight idle minutes.

**English and Russian.** One switch changes both the interface and the
recognition language. No auto-guessing, no mixed output.

**Your shortcut, your rules.** Hold Fn, hold Right Option, use a key combination,
or record any key. Choose hold-to-talk or hands-free toggle.

**Looks like it belongs.** Five themes, a tiny floating pill, and four subtle
start sounds.

### How it works

1. Press your shortcut.
2. Speak naturally.
3. Release. The text lands where your cursor is.

### Privacy

Hush has no networking code except the one-time model download. No account, no
cloud sync, no analytics. History stays in your Application Support folder, and
you can clear it at any time.

### Install

1. Download the DMG and open it.
2. Drag Hush into Applications.
3. Allow Microphone and Accessibility.
4. Download the speech model once.

*Hush is signed but not notarized yet. On first launch, right-click Hush → Open,
or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Hush.app`.*

### FAQ

**Does it work offline?** Yes. After the one-time model download, transcription
runs entirely on-device.

**Where does my audio go?** Nowhere. It is kept in memory, transcribed locally,
and discarded after the text is produced.

**Which Macs are supported?** macOS 14 or newer on Apple Silicon. Intel builds
are not provided.

**Can I use it without an internet connection on first launch?** You need a
connection once to download the model.

**Why does macOS ask for Accessibility?** Hush needs it to listen for the global
shortcut and to paste text into other apps.

### Частые вопросы

**Работает офлайн?** Да. После одной загрузки модели всё распознавание идёт на
устройстве.

**Куда уходит аудио?** Никуда. Оно хранится в памяти, распознаётся локально и
выбрасывается после получения текста.

**Какие Mac поддерживаются?** macOS 14 или новее на Apple Silicon. Сборки для
Intel нет.

**Нужен ли интернет при первом запуске?** Да, один раз — чтобы скачать модель.

**Зачем macOS просит Accessibility?** Чтобы ловить глобальный хоткей и вставлять
текст в другие приложения.
