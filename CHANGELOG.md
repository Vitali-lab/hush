# Changelog

## 1.2.1 — 2026-11-20

- The floating pill is a solid black capsule in dark themes and a solid white
  capsule in light themes, with no gradient.
- The waveform and status icons are pure monochrome to match.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-20

- Minimal 124×36 floating pill with a live waveform and no timer.
- Four selectable start sounds: Chime, Bloom, Pluck, Tick.
- English and Russian interface; recognition language follows the UI.
- Any key or modifier can be recorded as the dictation shortcut.
- Five themes: System, Daylight, Sand, Graphite, Midnight.
- Searchable history, custom words, snippets, and filler-word cleanup.
- Fixed the pill not appearing after starting from the popover or hiding the app.
- Fixed duplicate model loads, instant retry after a failed dictation, and idle
  model unloading.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-16

- Renamed the app to Hush.
- Added EN/RU interface and recognition, custom shortcuts, and themes.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-16

- First release: menu-bar dictation, local Whisper large-v3 Turbo, HUD,
  history, dictionary, snippets.
